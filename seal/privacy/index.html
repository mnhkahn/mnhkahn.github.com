Seal privacy policy

Seal is an Android app from Cyeam for recognizing Chinese seal script and looking up Chinese characters. Seal does not offer an app account.

Recognition, feedback and lookup

  • Seal crops photos and prepares handwritten character images on your device. When you submit a character for recognition, the app sends that image over HTTPS to Cyeam. Cyeam forwards it to its recognition service hosted on ModelScope and returns the recognition candidates.
  • Cyeam may keep the submitted image in private ImageKit storage with recognition results, submission time, request ID, IP address and app or device information from the request. We use these records to diagnose recognition problems and evaluate and improve recognition. If you send feedback, we record your choice and any selected character with the recognition record. Selected records may be annotated and exported for recognition evaluation and model improvement.
  • When you look up a character, Seal sends the query to Cyeam and requests reference images from Cloudinary or, when needed to display characters, GlyphWiki. These services receive the information needed to respond, such as the requested image and network address. Stroke-order and component data packaged with Seal can be viewed on the device.
  • Seal stores up to 50 recognition records on your device, with an aggregate limit of 32 MB for images. You can delete individual records or clear History. These actions remove the app’s local history.

App verification, trial and purchase

The Google Play version uses Firebase App Check with Play Integrity. Google processes app and device integrity information to issue a short-lived token. Seal sends the token to Cyeam to authorize recognition requests and help prevent abuse.

Seal sends the app-scoped Android device identifier to Cyeam to enforce the recognition trial. Cyeam stores a hash of the identifier and trial usage. Google Play processes payment and provides Seal a purchase token. Cyeam uses it to verify, acknowledge and restore the unlock with Google Play, and briefly caches the verification result in memory.

Usage analytics

When usage analytics is enabled, Google Firebase Analytics receives app events, an app-instance identifier, device information and approximate geography derived from the connection. Analytics may also receive Google Play purchase events with product and price information. We use this information to understand how the app is used. Seal’s recognition analytics events can include counts and candidate ranks. They do not include the submitted image, recognized characters, filenames or image links. You can turn off usage analytics in Seal’s Settings.

Retention and deletion

Cyeam’s private OCR debugging records have no fixed automatic expiration. We retain them while they are used for troubleshooting, evaluation or recognition improvement and remove identifiable records when they are no longer needed or in response to a verified deletion request, subject to any applicable retention obligations. Analytics data is managed under the retention controls for Cyeam’s Google Analytics property.

To request deletion of identifiable data held by Cyeam, email lichao@cyeam.com. Include the approximate recognition time and time zone, and the character if known. Please avoid attaching the original image in your first message. We may ask for limited additional details to confirm that a record is yours and distinguish it from other users’ records. After verification, we remove the matching identifiable records we control, including any identifiable copies we can locate, unless we are required to retain them. Clearing the app’s History does not send a server deletion request.

Questions about this policy can be sent to the same address.

Seal 隐私政策

Seal 是 Cyeam 提供的 Android 篆书识别与汉字查字应用。Seal 不提供应用账号注册。

识别、反馈与查字

  • Seal 在本机裁剪照片并生成手写字图。你发起识别时，应用通过 HTTPS 将提交的字图发送给 Cyeam。Cyeam 将字图转交托管在 ModelScope 上的识别服务，并返回识别候选。
  • 为排查识别问题、评估及改进识别效果，Cyeam 可能将提交的字图、识别结果、上传时间、请求编号、IP 地址和请求中的应用或设备信息保存在 ImageKit 私有存储。你提交识别反馈时，反馈选择和候选字会记录到对应识别记录。选中的记录可能被标注并导出，用于评估和改进识别模型。
  • 查字时，Seal 将所查的字发送给 Cyeam，并向 Cloudinary 请求字库图片。设备缺少所需字形时，应用也可能向 GlyphWiki 请求字形。这些服务会收到处理请求所需的信息，如所请求的图片和网络地址。应用随附的笔顺和部件资料可在本机查看。
  • Seal 在设备上最多保存 50 条识别历史，图片合计最多 32 MB。你可以在「历史」中逐条删除或清空，移除应用中的本机历史。

应用验证、免费试用与购买

Google Play 版使用 Firebase App Check 和 Play Integrity。Google 处理应用及设备完整性信息，签发短期凭证。Seal 将该凭证发送给 Cyeam，用于验证识别请求和防止滥用。

Seal 将应用范围内的 Android 设备标识发送给 Cyeam，用于计算免费识别次数。Cyeam 保存该标识的哈希值和试用用量。Google Play 处理付款并向 Seal 提供购买凭证。Cyeam 使用该凭证向 Google Play 核验、确认和恢复买断权益，并在内存中短暂缓存核验结果。

使用统计

开启使用统计时，Google Firebase Analytics 会收到应用互动事件、应用实例标识、设备信息，以及从网络连接推导的大致地区。使用统计也可能收到包含商品和价格信息的 Google Play 购买事件。Cyeam 使用这些信息了解应用的使用情况。Seal 的识别统计事件可以包含数量和候选排名，不包含提交字图、识别文字、文件名或图片链接。你可以在 Seal 的设置中关闭使用统计。

保留与删除

Cyeam 的私有 OCR 调试记录没有固定的自动到期时间。记录会在排查问题、评估或改进识别所需期间留存。记录不再需要时，或收到并核实删除请求后，Cyeam 会删除可识别的相关记录，适用规定要求保留的情况除外。统计数据按 Cyeam 的 Google Analytics 属性所配置的留存设置管理。

如需删除由 Cyeam 持有、能对应到你的数据，请发邮件至 lichao@cyeam.com，提供大概的识别日期、时间和时区。如果知道识别的字，也可以一并告知。首次联系请尽量不要附原图。为核实记录属于你，并与其他用户的记录区分，Cyeam 可能请你补充必要信息。核实后，Cyeam 会删除能定位的匹配记录和可识别的副本，适用规定要求保留的情况除外。清空本机历史不会发出服务端删除请求。

如对本政策有疑问，也可以通过该邮箱联系 Cyeam。
